BATTLE SCHOOL

A 3D game inspired by Ender's Game

Battle SchoolThe Battle Room

Zero-g laser tag in a 40 m cube. Command Dragon Army in first person: push off the stars, freeze the enemy with your flash gun, and reach their gate.

Command SchoolEnder's Console

Sit at the simulator. The battle is a hologram above the table: command your squadrons in 3D and use the Little Doctor when the swarm packs together.

The Third InvasionIn the War

Fly a fighter in the same battles. Your squadron fights around you. Press Tab any time to go back to the console and command.

A fan-made tribute to Orson Scott Card's novel Ender's Game (1985). It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the author or publisher. On a Leia display in the DisplayXR Browser everything is glasses-free 3D; any other browser shows it in 2D.
